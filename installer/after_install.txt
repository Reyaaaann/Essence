Essence is installed to:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Essence.vst3

To use it in FL Studio:
  1. Open FL Studio > Options > Manage plugins
  2. Click "Find installed plugins" (make sure the VST3 folder above is in the scan list)
  3. In the Plugin database, find Essence and add it to your favorites
  4. Channel Rack > + > Essence

180 sounds: pick a category tab, then click a sound. FL's preset arrows also step through all 180.
